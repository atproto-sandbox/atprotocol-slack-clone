Executive summary

R13 ships opensocial.group governance, 86/86 browser E2E gates, and an Operator-free Desk client in one open repo

  1. Workspaces are Group DIDs on apps/group-host with flat RBAC, cross-PDS invites/self, and ATProto-native moderation.
  2. Messages stay on member PDS repos; the Operator orchestrates admin mutations and content-free change hints only.
  3. Flock Desk (apps/desk, @flock/shell) matches canonical Web state without @flock/client or the Operator.

Part 1

Why

Why

Workspaces should be portable, permissioned, and readable without our servers

  • No app message database. Channel posts live on writer PDSes; group state on the Group Host.
  • Permission follows lexicons. RBAC and space credentials gate reads—not a shadow ACL.
  • Anyone can verify. @flock/reader and Flock Desk rebuild canonical state from public APIs alone.

The bet

A credible team app plus a second client proves Spaces are ready for real products—not only demos.

Flock still uses a small Operator for journaled admin work and change cursors—without storing message bodies or DM rows.

Flock Desk

Flock Desk is a genuinely independent browser client backed by @flock/shell on port 3005

  • Personal-PDS OAuth with deskMemberScopes() only—no Operator scopes in client metadata.
  • Direct writes and bounded reads; lifecycle journal flock-desk-lifecycle:<did> for join/leave only.
  • AST + ESLint boundary tests forbid @flock/client, @flock/web, and @flock/operator.
