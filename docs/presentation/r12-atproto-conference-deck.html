Flock · atproto Spaces

Team chat can live on the open web without a vendor-owned database

Flock is an alpha Slack-style workspace where channels and membership live on members’ PDSes. This talk is about why we built it that way and how we stay aligned as Spaces evolves.

Part 1

Why

Why

Workspaces should be portable, permissioned, and readable without our servers

  • No app database of record. Workspace state is spaces + member lists on each person’s host.
  • Permission follows the protocol. If you’re on the member list, you can read; the app doesn’t re-decide.
  • Anyone can verify. Our independent reader rebuilds workspaces from lexicons and PDS data alone.

The bet

A credible team app on ATProto proves Spaces are ready for real products—not only demos.

Apps still need a small operator: someone trusted to apply membership changes safely and tell clients when repos change—without holding the messages.

Why now

Spaces are moving fast; app builders need a reference that tracks upstream

Alpha

Targeting ATProto Spaces proposal work (not a frozen release)

We expect breakage—and plan for it

68

Browser scenarios exercise the product every CI run

Plus protocol acceptance tests

Open

Lexicons, operator, and tests are in the repo
