### Medium — 2

1. **Expired ownership is not fenced before authoritative mutation.** After heartbeat failures or a pause longer than the 15-second lease, another worker can claim the lease while the old hub remains able to accept and acknowledge wakes until its next successful heartbeat. Fence every authoritative mutation against the current lease token/expiry and step down when ownership cannot be proven.

2. **Configured IPC failure silently degrades to isolated authority.** A handshake timeout, wrong key, or exhausted election can leave `ipcState` as `none`, after which the worker acknowledges a local-only wake. Persistent-database mode must retry or return a retryable failure; local authority should be reserved for intentionally IPC-free configurations such as `:memory:`.
