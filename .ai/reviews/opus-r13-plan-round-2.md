## Medium

**M1. Outbound `notifyCredentialsRevoked` audience is unspecified, and the plan's symmetric inbound rule models a form the pinned receiver rejects.**
Plan:144 queues "retryable `com.atproto.space.notifyCredentialsRevoked` calls to active writers' PDSes" with no aud/iss/lxm row, while plan:104 states the group host's own inbound rule as `aud === hostedGroupDid || "${hostedGroupDid}#atproto_pds"`. The pinned receiver — the only party these emergency notices must interop with — accepts **only a bare hosted-account DID**: `isValidDid(aud)` followed by `getAccount(aud)` (pinned `packages/pds/src/api/com/atproto/space/notifyCredentialsRevoked.ts:13-27`; `packages/syntax/src/did.ts:67-71` regex rejects `#` fragments). An implementer following the plan's suffix-tolerant convention will mint `${writerDid}#atproto_pds` and every revocation notice will be rejected with `ForbiddenError`, silently retried, and dropped — defeating the takedown/emergency-revocation path (the plan's one justification for the `revoked_credential` machinery) and leaving revoked credentials honored for their full lifetime. Gate 5's test list (plan:232) also never exercises revocation fan-out.
**Fix:** add one row to the matrix: outbound `notifyCredentialsRevoked` uses `iss = groupDid`, `lxm = com.atproto.space.notifyCredentialsRevoked`, and `aud = <writerDid>` (bare account DID, per the pinned receiver); add a Gate 5 case asserting a revoked reader gets `CredentialRevoked` from a real member PDS.

**M2. The outbound `notifySpaceUpdate` union emission rules dropped two of four variants that Round 1 explicitly required.**
Pinned `lexicons/com/atproto/space/notifySpaceUpdate.json` defines `repoWrite`, `repoRemoved`, `#spaceDeleted`, and `#registrationExpired`. Rev 2 names only `#repoRemoved` (plan:19) and lists `notifySpaceUpdate` generically as an outbound call (plan:113) — Opus H2's remediation ("outbound notifySpaceUpdate with the `#spaceDeleted`/`#registrationExpired` union variants") was not carried over. Both variants gate flows the plan itself introduces: `deleteSpace` on extension/app teardown and sole-member group deactivation (plan:141 "revoking … notify registrations") need `#spaceDeleted`, and the registration-expiry lifecycle whose rejection-drop rule the plan borrows at plan:128 (mirroring pinned `space-notifications.ts:328-335`, which drops registrations that reject *their expiry notice*) needs `#registrationExpired` to exist at all. Under the plan's own "anything unlisted will silently not exist" standard, the reference host and `FakeNetwork` would ship without them and every gate would still pass, while pinned-convention syncers silently miss space deletions and post-expiry updates.
**Fix:** add one sentence to §3.1/§3.2 enumerating emission: `repoWrite` on each synced commit, `#repoRemoved` on writer eviction, `#spaceDeleted` on `deleteSpace` and group deactivation, `#registrationExpired` in place of the first post-expiry update; mirror in `FakeNetwork` and cover deletion + expiry notices in Gate 5.
